◆ link_information

typedef uint16_t* link_information

Holds 0:as_no, 1:egress_intf_no, 2:remote_as_no and 3:remote_ingress_intf_no.

We use a small int to store some identifying information on the links used in the beacons. Expected order of information:

  • link_info[0] = AS_number
  • link_info[1] = egress_interface_number;
  • link_info[2] = remote_as_number;
  • link_info[3] = remote_ingress_interface_number;
